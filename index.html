🧹 Cleaner

Independent Contractor Application & Agreement

Contractor Information
Cleaner Contractor Agreement

1. Services

The Contractor may provide residential cleaning, move-in/move-out cleaning, turnover cleaning, deep cleaning, kitchen and bathroom cleaning, floor care, trash removal and other agreed cleaning services.

2. Work Standards

Services must be performed professionally, safely and according to the agreed scope of work.

3. Payment

The parties will agree on the applicable service rate or project price before work begins.

4. Property Access

Keys, access codes and property information must be kept confidential and used only for authorized work.

5. Documentation

The Contractor may be asked to provide photographs or written confirmation of completed work and report property issues discovered during service.

6. Independent Contractor

The Contractor is engaged as an independent contractor and is responsible for their own taxes, licenses, permits, insurance and business expenses to the extent required by applicable law.

7. Safety & Property Care

The Contractor agrees to exercise reasonable care and promptly report damage, hazards or other significant property issues.

8. Termination

Either party may terminate the working relationship subject to applicable written agreements and law.

🔧 Handyman

Independent Contractor Application & Agreement

Contractor Information
Handyman Contractor Agreement

1. Services

The Contractor may provide general property maintenance, minor repairs, installations, painting, drywall work, fixture replacement, hardware installation, inspections and other specifically approved services.

2. Authorization

Work outside the agreed scope requires authorization. Work requiring permits or regulated professional services must comply with applicable law.

3. Materials

Material purchases and reimbursements should be approved before purchase unless otherwise agreed in writing.

4. Payment

Labor rates, project prices and payment terms will be agreed upon before work begins.

5. Property Access

Keys, access codes and property information must remain confidential and may only be used for authorized work.

6. Documentation

The Contractor may be required to provide photographs, receipts and descriptions of completed work.

7. Independent Contractor

The Contractor is engaged as an independent contractor and is responsible for their own taxes, licenses, permits, insurance and business expenses to the extent required by applicable law.

8. Safety & Compliance

The Contractor agrees to perform work safely and comply with applicable requirements.

9. Termination

Either party may terminate the working relationship subject to applicable written agreements and law.
