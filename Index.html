🧹 Cleaner

Independent Contractor Application & Agreement

Contractor Information
Cleaner Contractor Agreement

1. Services

The Contractor may provide residential cleaning, move-in/move-out cleaning, turnover cleaning, deep cleaning, surface cleaning, bathroom and kitchen cleaning, floor care, trash removal and other cleaning services agreed upon by the parties.

2. Work Standards

Contractor agrees to perform services professionally, safely, carefully and in accordance with the property owner's instructions and any agreed scope of work.

3. Payment

The parties will agree upon the rate, service fee or project price before work begins. Payment is subject to completion of the agreed scope of work.

4. Property Access

Keys, access codes and other property-access information must be kept confidential and used only for authorized work.

5. Documentation

Contractor may be required to provide photographs or written confirmation showing completed work and reporting maintenance issues discovered during service.

6. Independent Contractor

The Contractor is engaged as an independent contractor and is responsible for their own taxes, licenses, permits, insurance and business expenses to the extent required by applicable law.

7. Safety & Property Care

Contractor agrees to exercise reasonable care and immediately report damage, hazards, leaks, security concerns or other material property issues discovered during the work.

8. Termination

Either party may end the working relationship subject to any applicable written agreement and applicable law.

🔧 Handyman

Independent Contractor Application & Agreement

Contractor Information
Handyman Contractor Agreement

1. Services

The Contractor may provide general property maintenance, minor repairs, installations, painting, drywall work, fixture replacement, hardware installation, property inspections and other services specifically approved by the parties.

2. Authorization

Contractor must obtain approval before performing work outside the agreed scope. Major repairs, regulated work or work requiring a permit must be handled in accordance with applicable laws and property-owner instructions.

3. Materials

Material purchases, reimbursements and limits should be approved before purchase unless otherwise agreed in writing.

4. Payment

The parties will establish labor rates, project prices or other payment terms before work begins. Additional work requires appropriate authorization.

5. Property Access

Keys, access codes and property information are confidential and may only be used for authorized work.

6. Documentation

Contractor may be required to provide photographs, receipts, descriptions of completed work and notice of any additional maintenance concerns.

7. Independent Contractor

The Contractor is engaged as an independent contractor and is responsible for their own taxes, licenses, permits, insurance and business expenses to the extent required by applicable law.

8. Safety & Compliance

Contractor agrees to perform work safely and comply with applicable building, electrical, plumbing, occupational and other requirements.

9. Termination

Either party may end the working relationship subject to any applicable written agreement and applicable law.
